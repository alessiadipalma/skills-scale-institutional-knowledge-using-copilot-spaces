# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

## UX Designers

### Role Summary
UX Designers advocate for the end-user throughout the project lifecycle. They collaborate with Product Managers to understand requirements, create prototypes and specifications, and ensure a consistent and usable user experience across all deliverables.

### Responsibilities
- Advocate for user needs and accessibility in design decisions
- Contribute to requirements and user stories from a usability perspective
- Create prototypes, wireframes, and UI specifications
- Conduct usability testing and iterate based on feedback
- Ensure design consistency and adherence to design systems
- Review implementation to verify spec alignment and user experience

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support burden through good design
- Ensure design decisions are informed by user research and data

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions with Product and Engineering
- Usability testing reports and insights
- Spec handoffs and implementation reviews
- Participation in retrospectives to capture UX learnings

### Key Interactions
- **Product Managers**: align on user research, requirements, and prioritization
- **Developers**: collaborate on feasibility, implementation details, and code reviews
- **QA**: partner on usability testing and acceptance criteria validation
- **Support Lead**: gather feedback on user pain points and recurring issues

---

## DevOps Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers ensure reliable, automated, and observable infrastructure and deployment processes. They enable development teams to release features safely and enable operations teams to maintain system health.

### Responsibilities
- Design, build, and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Automate infrastructure provisioning and deployment processes
- Monitor production health, performance, and reliability
- Manage deployment processes and coordinate release activities
- Respond to release issues and production incidents
- Work with teams to establish runbooks and incident response procedures

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe, and reliable deployments
- Minimize production downtime and mean time to recovery (MTTR)
- Maintain observability and proactive alerting

### Typical Communication
- Release coordination meetings with Project Managers and Developers
- Incident response and post-mortems
- Deployment status and health dashboards
- Runbook documentation and training

### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: coordinate on deployment processes, test environment stability, and CI/CD improvements
- **Project Managers**: align on release windows, rollback plans, and deployment readiness
- **QA**: ensure test environments are properly configured and maintained
- **Support Lead**: collaborate on incident response and production health monitoring

---

## Support Lead

### Role Summary
Support Leads manage customer-facing issues and feedback. They serve as the voice of the customer within the team, triaging problems, identifying trends, and ensuring rapid resolution of critical issues.

### Responsibilities
- Manage intake and triage of customer-reported issues and feature requests
- Identify and escalate critical bugs and production incidents
- Communicate recurring pain points and usage patterns back to Product and Engineering
- Coordinate readiness for feature launches and rollouts
- Participate in incident response and root cause analysis
- Track and report on customer satisfaction and support metrics

### Goals
- Resolve customer issues quickly and thoroughly
- Identify and communicate user needs and pain points
- Reduce repeat issues through better product quality and documentation
- Maintain customer confidence and satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Incident reports and escalations
- Customer feedback summaries and trend analysis
- Launch readiness checklists and support documentation
- Participation in retrospectives to share customer impact

### Key Interactions
- **Product Managers**: report customer feedback trends, inform prioritization, and discuss roadmap alignment
- **Developers**: escalate bugs, provide reproduction details, and gather context for fixes
- **QA**: collaborate on acceptance testing, regression risk identification, and launch verification
- **Project Managers**: coordinate release rollouts, manage customer communication, and handle incident response
- **DevOps Engineers**: collaborate on production health monitoring, incident response, and deployment communications
